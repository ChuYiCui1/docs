description: "Access quarterly and annual cash flow statements including operating, investing, and financing activities, and free cash flow for A-share companies."
---

The `cash_flow` method returns standardised cash flow statement (现金流量表) data for A-share companies. Each row represents one reporting period and organises cash movements into the three standard sections: operating activities (经营活动), investing activities (投资活动), and financing activities (筹资活动). A pre-computed `free_cashflow` field is included for convenience. Like the other financial statement endpoints, `report_type` and `update_flag` help you identify amended and latest-revision filings.

```python
client.cash_flow(
    symbol: str | None = None,
    start_date: str | None = None,
    end_date: str | None = None,
    period: str | None = None,
    ann_date: str | None = None,
    f_ann_date: str | None = None,
    report_type: str | None = None,
    comp_type: str | None = None,
) -> pd.DataFrame
```

## Response Fields

### Identifiers & Report Metadata

<ResponseField name="symbol" type="str">
  Stock code in suffixed format, e.g. `"600519.SH"`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ann_date" type="date | None">
  Public announcement date of this filing (`YYYYMMDD`).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="f_ann_date" type="date | None">
  Formal filing announcement date (`YYYYMMDD`).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="end_date" type="date">
  Reporting period end date, e.g. `"20231231"` for the full-year statement.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="report_type" type="str">
  Report type code: `"1"` = regular, `"2"` = correction, `"3"` = adjustment.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="comp_type" type="str">
  Company type for accounting template: `"1"` = general, `"2"` = bank, `"3"` = securities, `"4"` = insurance.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="update_flag" type="str">
  `"1"` if this is the latest revision for the period; `"0"` if superseded.
</ResponseField>

### Operating Activities

<ResponseField name="net_profit" type="float">
  Net profit for the period (净利润), starting line of the indirect method reconciliation, in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_fr_sale_sg" type="float">
  Cash received from the sale of goods and rendering of services (销售商品、提供劳务收到的现金), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_pay_goods_purch_serv_rec" type="float">
  Cash paid for goods purchased and services received (购买商品、接受劳务支付的现金), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="n_cashflow_act" type="float">
  Net cash flow from operating activities (经营活动产生的现金流量净额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

### Investing Activities

<ResponseField name="c_pay_acq_const_fix_intang_oasset" type="float">
  Cash paid to acquire/construct fixed assets, intangible assets, and other long-term assets (购建固定资产、无形资产和其他长期资产支付的现金 / capex), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_fr_disp_fix_intang_oasset" type="float">
  Cash received from disposal of fixed assets, intangible assets, and other long-term assets (处置固定资产、无形资产和其他长期资产收回的现金款净额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="n_cashflow_inv_act" type="float">
  Net cash flow from investing activities (投资活动产生的现金流量净额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

### Financing Activities

<ResponseField name="c_fr_borr" type="float">
  Cash received from borrowings (取得借款收到的现金), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_pay_dist_dpcp_int_exp" type="float">
  Cash paid for dividends, profits distributed to minority owners, and interest (分配股利、利润或偿付利息支付的现金), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="n_cash_flows_fnc_act" type="float">
  Net cash flow from financing activities (筹资活动产生的现金流量净额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

### Summary

<ResponseField name="n_incr_cash_cash_equ" type="float">
  Net increase in cash and cash equivalents for the period (现金及现金等价物净增加额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_cash_equ_beg_period" type="float">
  Cash and cash equivalents at the beginning of the period (期初现金及现金等价物余额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="c_cash_equ_end_period" type="float">
  Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the period (期末现金及现金等价物余额), in CNY.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="free_cashflow" type="float">
  Free cash flow — `n_cashflow_act` minus capex (`c_pay_acq_const_fix_intang_oasset`), in CNY. Pre-computed for convenience.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  All monetary values are in **CNY (yuan)**. Cash outflows are represented as **negative** numbers in the raw CSRC filings, so `c_pay_goods_purch_serv_rec` and `c_pay_acq_const_fix_intang_oasset` will typically carry negative signs.
</Note>

<Tip>
  A company's **operating cash conversion quality** can be assessed by dividing `n_cashflow_act` by `net_profit`. A ratio consistently above 1.0 indicates high-quality earnings; ratios well below 1.0 may signal aggressive accrual accounting.
</Tip>

## Example

```python
from asharehub import AShareHub

client = AShareHub(api_key="ash_...")

# Annual cash flow statements for CATL (300750.SZ) — last 5 years
df = client.cash_flow(
    symbol="300750.SZ",
    start_date="20190101",
    end_date="20231231",
)

# Filter to latest revision of annual reports
annual = df[
    (df["end_date"].str.endswith("12-31")) &
    (df["update_flag"] == "1")
].copy()

# Cash conversion quality
annual["cash_conversion"] = annual["n_cashflow_act"] / annual["net_profit"]

print(annual[[
    "end_date",
    "n_cashflow_act",
    "n_cashflow_inv_act",
    "n_cash_flows_fnc_act",
    "free_cashflow",
    "cash_conversion",
]])
```
